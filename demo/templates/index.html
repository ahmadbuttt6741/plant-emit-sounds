🔬 Botanical Methodology & Diagnostic Boundary

PhytoSound AI provides bio-acoustic screening heuristics based on plant stem micro-vibrations and cavitation physics. Acoustic alerts indicate mechanical and hydraulic vascular activity and should be verified against ground-truth physiological sensors (e.g. soil tensiometers, stomatal conductance porometers, or Scholander pressure bombs) before making definitive agronomic intervention decisions.
